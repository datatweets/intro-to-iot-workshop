# Introduction to the Internet of Things: Workshop Materials

**Build a working smart device in one day, from your web browser.**
No electronics kit, no software to install, and no coding experience needed.

This repository has everything you need for the workshop: step-by-step lab
guides, ready-to-run code, the slides for every module, and the course book.

---

## What you'll build

Across the day you build a **smart room monitor**, one piece at a time. By the
end, it:

- measures the temperature and humidity of a room,
- shows the live readings on a small screen,
- switches on a fan by itself when the room gets too hot,
- and sends its readings to the internet, so you can watch them from anywhere.

You build it in a free online simulator called **Wokwi**. It runs a real
ESP32 (a small, low-cost computer used in many smart devices) inside your
browser. The same code would work on real hardware.

## Start here, in 3 steps

1. **Set up your free accounts** (about 10 minutes):
   follow [Getting started](1-getting-started/).
2. **Open the lab** your trainer names, in [Labs](2-labs/). Each lab has a
   PDF guide with a picture for every step, and the code files you'll paste
   into the simulator.
3. **Follow the slides and the book** for the ideas behind each lab:
   [Slides](3-slides/) and [Book](4-book/).

## What you need

- A laptop with an up-to-date web browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).
- An internet connection.
- Two free accounts, Wokwi and ThingSpeak. [Getting started](1-getting-started/)
  shows you how to make them.

That's all. You don't need to buy or install anything.

## How to use the files

- **To read a guide**, click its PDF. GitHub shows it right in your browser,
  and the download button (top right of the preview) saves a copy.
- **To use the code**, open the lab's folder, click a file such as
  `sketch.ino`, then click the **Copy raw file** button (two overlapping
  squares, top right of the code). Paste it into Wokwi as the lab guide shows.
- **To get everything at once**, click the green **Code** button at the top
  of this page, then **Download ZIP**.
